About Us

Holyghost Family Life Centre Inc. is a place where lives are transformed through the Word, worship, and the presence of the Holy Spirit. We are committed to helping families grow in faith, love, and purpose through Christ Jesus.

Our mission is to reach the world with the gospel, restore broken lives, and raise champions for the Kingdom.
